01The problem

The container is one of the most heavily regulated parts on an FSAE car. It has to hold and protect the battery, meet the competition’s structural and safety rules, and mount solidly to the chassis. Every one of those requirements pulls toward more material.

02What I did

  • Redesigned the container through iterative modeling in Autodesk Inventor, saving 1 kg: 10% of the previous design’s mass.
  • Pioneered the use of composite materials for the container on the team.
  • Created GD&T-compliant engineering drawings for the battery mounting hardware.
  • Machined clevises, bushings, and other aluminum mounting parts on a mill and lathe.
  • Manufactured the redesigned container for the car.
