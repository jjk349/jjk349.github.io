01The idea

DCIR, or direct-current internal resistance, is found by stepping the current through a cell and recording how far its voltage drops. The ratio of the two is the cell’s resistance, and the heat it produces under load scales with I²R. That makes it the link between the battery’s electrical design and the cooling system that has to carry the heat away.

02What I did

  • Performed internal resistance testing on lithium-polymer pouch cells.
  • Used the results to quantify how much heat the cells generate.
  • Applied those numbers to validate the battery pack architecture, cooling, and BMS configuration.
