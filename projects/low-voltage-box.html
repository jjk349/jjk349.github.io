Low Voltage Box

The ARG25 low-voltage box houses the electronics that control and monitor the car’s high-voltage system. I designed the enclosure and took it from CAD to the car.

01The problem

The box has to package a defined set of low-voltage electrical components into the space available on the car.

02What I did

  • Designed the enclosure in CAD around the required electrical components.
  • 3D-printed prototypes to check fit and packaging before committing to final parts.
  • Manufactured the final components and integrated them onto the car.
